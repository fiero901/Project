1. What our project is?

Guardian AI Companion Cube is the world's first AI Sign Language Tutor Cube – a focused accessibility device enabling two‑way communication between deaf and hearing people.

Why we built it: In Nepal, there are over 500,000 people with hearing disabilities, yet very few affordable, AI‑powered communication aids. Guardian Cube bridges this gap using locally available components and open‑source AI.

Physical structure

  • 15cm × 15cm × 15cm perfect cube
  • 1.3 kg · 3D printed PLA + acrylic
  • 6000mAh Li-Po · 10h runtime
  • 360° motorized rotating base
  • Haptic feedback (2 motors)
  • 60 RGB matrix + 30 LED strip

3. Features

A. Two‑way sign

  • Deaf→hearing: sign to speech real-time
  • Hearing→deaf: speech to animated skeleton
  • 21‑pt MediaPipe, ASL + 20 Nepali signs
  • Custom sign creation & finger error detection
  • Instant replay, accuracy score 0‑100%

B. Sign tutor

  • Side‑by‑side user vs correct skeleton
  • Slow‑motion replay of correct form
  • “Sign of the day” on idle LED
  • Gesture‑based login (secret hand sign)

C. Health snapshot

  • Heart rate via facial rPPG (10s capture)
  • Stress level: voice tone + micro-expressions
  • Non‑medical, demo only – disclaimer shown

D. Gesture + LED memory

  • Cube flashes 2‑5 step LED pattern
  • User repeats via hand signs OR arcade buttons
  • “Can you beat the cube?” adaptive difficulty
  • Daily top‑3 leaderboard (resets daily)

E. Viral QR + selfie

  • Post‑game visitor snapshot (auto face crop)
  • Unique QR → download photo + “I beat Guardian!”
  • Auto #GuardianCube, 5‑min auto-delete

F. Exhibition + silent

  • Auto‑demo (person wakes cube, 2min cycle)
  • One‑button 30s instant demo
  • Silent mode: zero voice, haptic+LED only
  • Visitor counter, lifetime interactions

G. Multi‑modal feedback: LED matrix (60+30) shows status; voice (TTS) for hearing; haptic (2 motors) for deaf; sound FX – success chime (C major), error tone, win/lose melodies, QR shutter.

H. Speaker/gesture awareness: Face detection identifies active user; 4‑mic array detects direction; visual highlight box around signer.

I. Environment snapshot: DHT22 (temp/humidity), MQ135 (air quality: good/moderate/poor). Simple, reliable, no forecasts.

J. System features: Gesture wake ("Hey Guardian" or wave); low‑battery survival mode (<10% disables camera AI, keeps game); 360° rotating base; 6 arcade buttons (nav, confirm, demo, silent).
